## ORIGIN

PROJECTKIDCREATIONS — **PKC** — operates on a simple thesis: **gear should be built, not bought.**

The mainstream gel-blaster scene is dominated by mass-manufactured imports. Sealed parts. Plastic-fantastic specs. Brand loyalty wired in by marketing, not by build quality. PKC was founded to flip that script. Every chassis, every modular kit, every aftermarket part lives as a 3D-printable file first. You print it on your own hardware, in your own livery, dialed in to your own loadout. The maker keeps control. The brand is the design language, not the supply chain.

PKC is not anti-purchase. PKC sells finished kits, sells drops, sells materials. But the **brand commits to making the file path possible alongside the buy path**. Every drop ships with the option to print yourself. That's the rebellion. Open source, productized.

## THE THESIS — "ENGINEERED REBELLION."

Two words. Read them carefully.

**Engineered.** Nothing here is hacked together. PKC files are designed to spec, dimensioned, tested, and documented. Tolerances are real. Materials are called out. Print orientation matters. The output is gear, not a prop. Engineering rigor is the floor.

**Rebellion.** The market doesn't want you to print your own. It wants you to buy, register, accessorize, and re-buy. PKC's existence as a viable alternative is an act of refusal. So is the community around it. So is every member who picks up a file, prints it, and runs it on the range.

The tagline isn't merch. It's the operating principle.

## WHO PKC SERVES

Three overlapping audiences, no hard lines between them:

**Operators** — people who run gel-blasters and want better gear than what's on the shelf. They came for the product.

**Makers** — people who print, mod, design, and remix. They came for the files.

**Collectors** — people who follow the drops, watch the limited editions, run liveries from past releases. They came for the brand.

The Discord server is where all three live together. The channel layout reflects that overlap — `#showcase` is for operators showing their loadouts AND makers showing their prints AND collectors showing their lineup. The roles reflect the overlap — @Verified Builder is for makers, @VIP is for collectors who support the brand, @New Shooter is for everyone who shows up consistently.

## DESIGN LANGUAGE

PKC's visual language is locked. Five colors. Two typefaces. Strict ratios. The design system lives in its own repo (`AlxanderArt/PKCDesignSystem`) and feeds everything PKC ships — product packaging, drop graphics, this Discord server, the bot.

- **Tactical black** `#0A0A0A` — base
- **Hi-vis orange** `#FF5F1F` — accent for in-progress / onboarding / active state
- **Anodized slate** `#3F4448` — secondary
- **Ghost grey** `#1A1C1E` — deep neutral
- **Concrete white** `#E8E8E8` — type

Plus a **conditional accent**:
- **Neon green** `#39FF14` — for verified / earned state only (badges, VIP role, certain product UI)

Type pair: **Archivo Black** for display (UPPERCASE ONLY), **JetBrains Mono** for body. Mono-as-body is the brand signature.

If you ever see PKC content that violates these — that's a leak or a fake, not us.

## WHY A DISCORD SERVER

A brand that ships open files needs a community that can ask questions, post builds, troubleshoot prints, and tag drops. Forums work for documentation. Reddit works for memes. Discord works for **building together in real time** — which is what PKC is about.

The server is canonical. If a build conversation isn't here, it didn't happen.

## WHAT YOU WON'T FIND HERE

To save you time:

- **Sales of competitors' products.** Partnerships go through staff. Random ads get deleted.
- **Real-firearm content.** PKC is gel-blasters only. We don't host or discuss real-firearm builds, modifications, or trade.
- **Pirated STLs.** If you don't have rights, don't post it. We protect designers — including ours.
- **NSFW anything.** The server is 14+. Marketing surfaces are 18+ gated. Across both, no NSFW content.
- **Political fights.** Channels have topics for a reason.
