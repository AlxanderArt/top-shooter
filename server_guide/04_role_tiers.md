Roles function as a **progression system**, not a permission grab-bag. New members enter as @Recruit. Roles above that are earned through participation (auto), deliverables (manual), or paid support.

## THE LADDER (top → bottom)

The hierarchy below is also the **display order** in the member list. Top roles render with hoisted color blocks; lower roles are flat.

### ⬛ Owner
- **Color:** hi-vis orange
- **Who:** Founder of PKC. There is one.
- **Perms:** Administrator.
- **How to earn:** You don't. There's one Owner.

### ⬛ Founder
- **Color:** tactical black
- **Who:** Inner-circle co-creators. Long-term partners, not employees.
- **Perms:** Administrator.
- **How to earn:** Invited by the Owner. Not applied-for.

### 🟥 Mod
- **Color:** alarm red
- **Who:** Server moderators. Handle reports, mod actions, audit log.
- **Perms:** Kick, ban, timeout, manage messages, manage threads.
- **How to earn:** Invitation only. Must already be @Verified Builder or @New Shooter with sustained contribution. Owner/Founder decides.

### ⬜ Designer
- **Color:** concrete white
- **Who:** PKC product team. People working on actual drops.
- **Perms:** Manage messages, manage threads, host Stage events. Limited.
- **How to earn:** Internal team only. Granted on hire / contractor agreement.

### 🟩 VIP
- **Color:** neon green (verified-state)
- **Who:** **Paying supporters.** Patreon backers, Ko-fi tippers, or members who've hit a lifetime PKC-store spend threshold ($100+ all-time).
- **Perms:** Standard member perms plus early-access privileges (VIP early-access tier in drop pipeline).
- **How to earn:** Subscribe / spend. Staff syncs roles periodically (manual until the integration ships). DM a @Mod with your order receipt or Patreon screenshot if not auto-synced.

### 🟧 Verified Builder
- **Color:** light orange
- **Who:** Members who have **passed the standing build challenge**.
- **Perms:** Can post threads in `#print-files`. Eligible for build-along prizes. Highlight features in `#showcase`.
- **How to earn:** Complete the standing PKC build challenge (see thread → **Build Pipeline** for the current challenge spec). Submit photos with PKC watermark. Mods review and grant. **No expiration** — once earned, kept.

### 🟪 Beta Tester
- **Color:** purple
- **Who:** Members enrolled in the PKC beta program — they get early access to unreleased drops for feedback.
- **Perms:** Beta Tester preview window on every drop (3 days before public).
- **How to earn:** Apply via staff once you're @Verified Builder. Active program is small — typically 10–20 testers per drop cycle. Rotation is at staff discretion.

### 🟦 New Shooter
- **Color:** Dodger blue
- **Who:** Active community members. The baseline "trusted member" role.
- **Perms:** Can post in @everyone-restricted channels (`#general`, `#showcase`, `#build-help`, `#mod-talk`, voice channels except Stage).
- **How to earn:** Two paths:
  1. **Top Shooter XP** — reach Level 20 by chatting (about ~1000 messages, weeks to months at a normal pace). Auto-granted.
  2. **Manual** — a Mod grants it earlier if they see consistent, high-quality contribution.

### ⚪ Recruit
- **Color:** none (default text)
- **Who:** Default role on join.
- **Perms:** Read-only on most channels. Can post **only in `#off-topic`** until promoted to @New Shooter. Can react and use slash commands in `#bot-commands`.
- **How to earn:** Just join.

## TAG ROLES (cosmetic + functional)

These don't sit in the hierarchy ladder. They're horizontal — anyone can have them based on onboarding answers or self-selection.

### Age
- `18+` — unlocks marketing-restricted content (per PKC brand policy, marketing can't target under-18).
- `14-17` — full community access, no marketing-targeted content.

### Build experience (onboarding Q4)
- `Builder · Newbie` — first build or no build yet.
- `Builder · Intermediate` — few builds in, comfortable with basics.
- `Builder · Expert` — designing your own mods.

### Ping preferences (onboarding Q3 + future self-serve menu)
- `Pings · Drops` — get pinged on every new drop.
- `Pings · Events` — get pinged for range meetups and Stage events.
- `Pings · Beta` — get pinged for beta-tester openings.
- `Pings · Silent` — opts out of every ping role.
- `Pings · Build Streams` — get pinged when staff goes live with a build walkthrough.
- `Pings · Mod Drops` — get pinged on aftermarket mod releases (sub-category of Drops).
- `Pings · Range Meetups` — get pinged on in-person event coordination.
- `Pings · Anniversary` — get pinged on PKC anniversary giveaways.

### Referral (onboarding Q5 — analytics only)
- `Found · Friend`, `Found · Social`, `Found · Video`, `Found · Search`, `Found · Event`. Used by staff to see where members are coming from. No perms attached.

### Identity (onboarding Q1)
- `Partner` — affiliate or partnership-relationship member. Staff verifies and grants.

## HOW PROMOTIONS WORK

**Auto promotions** (handled by Top Shooter):
- Hit XP Level 20 → @New Shooter granted automatically. You'll see a level-up message and the role appears.

**Manual promotions** (handled by staff):
- @Verified Builder — after passing the standing challenge, DM a @Mod with the challenge submission. Mod reviews and grants.
- @VIP — automatic if syncing from Patreon/store (when integration ships). Manual now: DM @Mod with proof of support.
- @Beta Tester — applied for through @Mod once @Verified Builder.
- @Designer / @Mod / @Founder / @Owner — invitation only.

**Demotions:**
- @New Shooter can be revoked for rule violations. Staff discretion.
- @Verified Builder is permanent unless you violate piracy / safety rules in BUILDS-MODS.
- @VIP expires if your supporting subscription lapses (with a 30-day grace period).

## ROLE PROGRESSION MAP

```
Recruit  →  New Shooter        →  Verified Builder  →  Beta Tester
            (XP Lvl 20)         (build challenge)     (apply when VB)

             │                    │
             ▼                    ▼
            VIP                  Designer / Mod / Founder
            (paying support)     (invitation only)
```

VIP and the hierarchy-promotion track are **parallel** — paying for VIP doesn't promote you up the staff ladder, and being staff doesn't auto-grant VIP. Different criteria, different roles.
