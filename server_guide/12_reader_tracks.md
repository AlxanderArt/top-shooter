# Reader Tracks — #server-guide thread 11

**Thread title:** `READER TRACKS — PICK YOUR PATH.`
**Tags:** `quickstart`, `navigation`
**Color:** `#FF5F1F` (hi-vis orange)

---

Different readers want different things out of PKC. This thread gives you a labeled path through the server based on what you're here for. Pick one. Or pick more than one. They're not exclusive.

---

## 🔍 TRACK 1 — "I'M JUST HERE TO LURK."

You're not building, not buying, just watching. Welcome — that's a valid mode.

**What you should do:**
1. Read pinned embeds in `#welcome` (5 minutes).
2. Pick up `Pings · Silent` in onboarding (no pings ever).
3. Set the server to **Notification Settings → Nothing** if you want a fully quiet experience.
4. Hang out in `#off-topic`. Drop reactions, not posts.

**What you'll see:**
- Drops happen monthly in `#drops`. Watch if you're curious.
- Showcase posts in `#showcase` if you want to see what the community is building.
- Brand story in `#server-guide` → Brand Story, if you ever want to understand why this place exists.

**What to skip:**
- The build pipeline. Verification challenge. The role ladder. All optional for lurkers.

**Time investment:** 5 minutes upfront, near-zero ongoing.

---

## 🛠️ TRACK 2 — "I WANT TO BUILD."

You print, mod, design, or want to start. This is the maker track and it goes deepest in the server.

**Step 1 — Get oriented**
- Read `#server-guide` → **Build Pipeline** (the canonical workflow).
- Read `#server-guide` → **Glossary** if you're new to gel-blaster scene terms or 3D-printing jargon.

**Step 2 — Pick up a file**
- Open `#print-files` (the Forum channel). Browse threads.
- Filter by tag: `FDM`, `Resin`, `WIP`, `Finished`, `Open-Source`, `PKC-Official`.
- Pick something that matches your printer and material lineup.

**Step 3 — Print it**
- Follow the print settings in the thread.
- If you hit problems, post in `#build-help` with photos + settings + material. Be specific.

**Step 4 — Post your build**
- Once it's printed, dialed in, and field-tested → post in `#showcase`.
- 1–4 photos. Source thread linked. Print report included.

**Step 5 — Earn @Verified Builder**
- Complete the **standing PKC build challenge** (spec in `#server-guide` → Build Pipeline → Stage 6).
- Submit photos in `#build-help` with `[VERIFY]` prefix.
- Mods review within 7 days. Pass → role granted.

**Step 6 — Keep going**
- Once @Verified Builder, post your own files as new threads in `#print-files`.
- Apply for @Beta Tester when staff opens a cycle.
- Eligible for build-along events (quarterly) and prize tiers.

**Pings to pick up:**
- `Pings · Drops` (to know when new files / kits release)
- `Pings · Build Streams` (live build walkthroughs)

**Time investment:** Days to first build. Weeks to @Verified Builder. Months to deep maker membership.

---

## 🔴 TRACK 3 — "I'M HERE FOR DROPS ONLY."

You collect, you watch limited editions, you want first-in-line. This is the drop-collector track.

**Step 1 — Read the calendar**
- `#server-guide` → **Drop Pipeline**. Understand the staging: Beta → VIP → public.

**Step 2 — Pick up drop pings**
- `Pings · Drops` (every drop, all formats)
- `Pings · Mod Drops` (accessories / aftermarket only — smaller drops on faster cycles)

**Step 3 — Decide if you want early access**
- If yes → become @VIP. Support PKC via Patreon, Ko-fi, or hit $100+ lifetime store spend. @VIP unlocks 24h early access to drops. (See `#server-guide` → Role Tiers.)
- If you also want files / hands-on participation → become @Verified Builder (Track 2 above). Verified Builders can apply for @Beta Tester, which unlocks **7 days** early access (vs VIP's 24h).

**Step 4 — Set up notifications**
- Right-click PROJECTKIDCREATIONS → Notification Settings → **Only @mentions** (so you only get pinged on Drops, not on every general chat).
- Or → **All Messages** if you want full immersion.

**Step 5 — Drop day**
- Pinged 48 hours before drop time.
- Drop opens at the announced time.
- Time-boxed — close window varies per drop, announced in the `#drops` post.
- Move fast on limited paid kits. Open files stay forever.

**Pings to pick up:**
- `Pings · Drops`
- `Pings · Mod Drops`
- `Pings · Anniversary` (annual giveaways)

**Time investment:** Minimal day-to-day. Hours of attention on drop days.

## ⭐ TRACK 4 — "I WANT TO BE STAFF."

You want to be a mod, designer, or part of the PKC team. **This track is the longest and the least direct.** Staff isn't applied-for; it's invited.

**Step 1 — Earn @New Shooter**
- Reach Top Shooter Level 20 (sustained chatting over weeks/months).
- Or get a mod to manually promote you for visibly high-quality contribution.

**Step 2 — Earn @Verified Builder**
- Complete the standing challenge. Demonstrates you can build to spec.
- Required for any of the upper tiers (Beta Tester, Designer consideration, eventual Mod).

**Step 3 — Contribute consistently**
- Help people in `#build-help`.
- Post quality builds in `#showcase`.
- Participate in `#mod-talk` discussions.
- Apply for @Beta Tester when a cycle opens — that's the first formal trust signal.

**Step 4 — Get noticed**
- @Mod is invited by Owner/Founder. They watch for: consistent helpfulness, brand alignment, calm under pressure, clean Discord track record (no rule violations).
- @Designer is invited from internal staff conversations. Has to be a maker who can ship PKC-compliant design work.
- @Founder is invited by Owner — long-term partners only.
- @Owner doesn't exist as an earn-path. There's one Owner.

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't ask to be staff. It's an anti-signal.
- Don't try to take over channels, mod-style. That's an anti-signal.
- Don't lead with your "credentials." Lead with what you've built.

**Time investment:** Months to a year minimum for any meaningful staff role.

---

## 🧑‍🎨 TRACK 5 — "I'M A DESIGNER / PARTNER."

You design products, run a content channel, or operate a parallel brand and want a relationship with PKC.

**Step 1 — Do NOT cold-DM Owner or Founder.**
**Step 2 — Pick up the @Partner tag** during onboarding (Q1, "Affiliate / partner" option) — this signals staff that you're partner-positioned.
**Step 3 — Post in `#general` once you're @New Shooter, briefly introducing yourself + what you do.**
**Step 4 — DM a @Mod** with: who you are, what you make, what kind of partnership you're proposing. They'll route to `#partnerships` (staff-only) for evaluation.
**Step 5 — Staff responds** when they get to it. PKC is selective on partnerships — typically a few per year, not a rolling intake.

If you're a creator who covers PKC drops on a channel, just keep doing the work. PKC notices.
