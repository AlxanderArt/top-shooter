# Channel Map — #server-guide thread 2

**Thread title:** `CHANNEL MAP — WHERE EVERYTHING LIVES.`
**Tags:** `navigation`
**Color:** `#FF5F1F` (hi-vis orange)

---

The server is organized into **seven categories**. Each category has a defined purpose; channels inside it inherit that purpose. Below is the full channel-by-channel reference.

---

## 📋 INFO — Public Read

The bulletin-board tier. Everyone can see, only staff can post.

### `#welcome`
- **Purpose:** Landing page. Pinned embed series explains the server.
- **Who posts:** Mods only.
- **What to do here:** Read the pinned embeds. Move on.

### `#rules`
- **Purpose:** Rules of Engagement. Canonical.
- **Who posts:** Mods only.
- **What to do here:** Read. React with the green check to acknowledge.

### `#announcements`
- **Purpose:** Official calls from command. Major server-wide announcements live here.
- **Who posts:** Mods only.
- **What to do here:** Read. No replies in-channel — discuss in `#general` or `#off-topic`.

### `#drops` *(Announcement channel)*
- **Purpose:** Product releases, new STL drops, restocks.
- **Who posts:** Staff only.
- **What to do here:** Watch. Pick up `Pings · Drops` to get notified. Follow this channel from other servers (it's a Discord Announcement channel — partners can mirror it).

---

## 🎯 COMMUNITY — Public Read, Mostly Gated Post

The conversation tier. Everyone can read; @New Shooter+ can post in most channels.

### `#general`
- **Purpose:** The barracks. General-purpose chat.
- **Who posts:** @New Shooter+ (earn via Level 20 or completing onboarding-derived role progression).
- **Slowmode:** 5 seconds.
- **What to do here:** Drop in once you have @New Shooter. Keep it civil. Brand-adjacent or off-topic is fine.

### `#showcase`
- **Purpose:** Show your work. Gear, builds, range photos, livery shots.
- **Who posts:** @New Shooter+. @Verified Builder gets priority for highlight features.
- **Slowmode:** 10 seconds.
- **What to do here:** Post one photo or thread per build. Tag what you're running. Credit STL designers.

### `#off-topic`
- **Purpose:** Everything else. Memes, side conversations, links.
- **Who posts:** **Everyone** (this is the ONE channel chattable for @everyone — your landing zone before onboarding completes).
- **Slowmode:** None.
- **What to do here:** Anything that isn't a rule break. Keep noise here, not in the focused channels.

---

## 🛠️ BUILDS-MODS — The Maker Zone

Where the brand thesis lives. @New Shooter+ can read; posting in `#print-files` requires @Verified Builder.

### `#print-files` *(Forum channel)*
- **Purpose:** STL / 3MF / STEP file drops. One build per thread.
- **Who posts:** @Verified Builder+ creates threads. @New Shooter+ can reply.
- **Format:** Title = build name. First post = file link + print settings + material recommendations. Tags: `FDM`, `Resin`, `WIP`, `Finished`, `Open-Source`, `PKC-Official`.
- **What to do here:** Post your file with full print settings. Comment in others' threads with print reports.

### `#build-help`
- **Purpose:** Stuck on a print or a build. Post here for help.
- **Who posts:** @New Shooter+.
- **What to do here:** Photos of the problem. Print settings. Material. What you tried. Be specific — vague help requests get vague answers.

### `#mod-talk`
- **Purpose:** Mod design discussion — talking about future mods, what would be cool, spec ideas, reference posts.
- **Who posts:** @New Shooter+.
- **What to do here:** Discuss design, not request products. (Drops are scheduled by the staff, not the chat.)

---

## 🤖 BOT — Top Shooter's Zone

Where the bot does its work.

### `#bot-commands`
- **Purpose:** Run slash commands here so the chat channels stay clean.
- **Slowmode:** 30 seconds (prevents command-spam).
- **Who posts:** @New Shooter+.
- **What to do here:** `/help` to see what's available. `/rank` for your level. `/leaderboard` for top contributors.

### `#bot-logs`
- **Purpose:** Top Shooter's automated activity log. Visible for transparency.
- **Who posts:** Bot only.
- **What to do here:** Read if you care. Don't reply.

---

## 🎙️ VOICE — Comms

### `PKC Stage` *(Stage channel)*
- **Purpose:** Official PKC events. AMAs, drop reveals, live build walkthroughs.
- **Format:** One speaker, listeners. Members request to speak via the icon.
- **Who can host:** @Mod+, @Founder, @Designer.
- **What to do here:** Show up when staff schedules a Stage. Otherwise leave it idle.

### `Range 1` and `Range 2`
- **Purpose:** General voice hangs.
- **Who joins:** @New Shooter+.
- **What to do here:** Hop in. Talk. Print together over voice.

### `+ NEW VOICE` *(temp-voice trigger)*
- **Purpose:** Join this channel to spawn your own temporary voice channel. The bot creates a new room under VOICE named after you. When the room empties, the bot deletes it.
- **What to do here:** Use it when Range 1 / Range 2 are busy and you want a private hang.

> **Note:** Temp voice requires Top Shooter Phase 10 to be live. Until then, join Range 1 / Range 2.

## 🔒 STAFF — Mod-Only

Not visible to members. For reference only:
- `#mod-chat` — staff comms
- `#audit-log` — bot-driven mod-action log
- `#planning` — staff planning, drop coordination
- `#drops-pipeline` — unreleased drop discussion
- `#partnerships` — affiliate / partner ops

If you think a conversation should be happening here that isn't, DM a @Mod.

## 👁️ FOUNDER — Owner-Only

Founder workspace. Not relevant to most members. Exists for the operator running the brand to think out loud.

---

## NAVIGATION TIPS

- The **server name dropdown** at the top of the channel list gives you Notifications, Server Boost, Edit Profile, and (after onboarding) **Edit Your Tags** to re-pick onboarding answers.
- The **gear icon** next to the server name (top-left of the channel list) opens **Server Settings** if you're staff. Members see a more limited version.
- The **# icon next to a channel name** indicates a regular text channel. **📢** is Announcement. **🔊** is voice. **🎤** is Stage. **🌳** is Forum.
- Channels with a **🔒 padlock** are locked to a role you don't have yet.
